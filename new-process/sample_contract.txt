SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 15, 2026
between PT Bank Digital Nusantara ("Client"), a banking institution
registered under the laws of the Republic of Indonesia, and PT CloudTech
Solutions ("Provider"), a technology services company registered in Jakarta.

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES

1.1 The Provider shall deliver an AI-powered Core Banking Middleware Platform
("Platform") including API gateway, transaction processing engine, and
customer data analytics module.

1.2 The Provider shall complete implementation within 12 months from the
Effective Date, with the following milestones:
- Phase 1 (Month 1-3): Infrastructure setup and API development
- Phase 2 (Month 4-8): Core module deployment and integration testing
- Phase 3 (Month 9-12): UAT, data migration, and go-live

2. PAYMENT TERMS

2.1 Total contract value: IDR 15,000,000,000 (fifteen billion Rupiah).

2.2 Payment schedule:
- 30% upon signing: IDR 4,500,000,000
- 30% upon Phase 2 completion: IDR 4,500,000,000
- 40% upon go-live acceptance: IDR 6,000,000,000

2.3 Late payment shall incur a penalty of 2% per month on the outstanding
amount, compounded monthly.

2.4 The Provider may suspend services if payment is overdue by more than
60 days without prior written notice to the Client.

3. SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT

3.1 The Platform shall maintain 99.5% uptime measured monthly, excluding
scheduled maintenance windows.

3.2 Scheduled maintenance shall not exceed 8 hours per month and must be
conducted between 00:00-06:00 WIB on weekends.

3.3 Response times:
- Critical incidents (P1): 15 minutes response, 4 hours resolution
- Major incidents (P2): 1 hour response, 8 hours resolution
- Minor incidents (P3): 4 hours response, 3 business days resolution

3.4 Service credits for SLA breaches:
- Uptime below 99.5% but above 99.0%: 5% of monthly fee
- Uptime below 99.0% but above 95.0%: 15% of monthly fee
- Uptime below 95.0%: 30% of monthly fee and Client may terminate

3.5 Service credit claims must be submitted within 30 days of the incident.

4. DATA PROTECTION AND SECURITY

4.1 The Provider shall process all Client data in compliance with applicable
Indonesian data protection laws and OJK regulations on banking data.

4.2 All Client data shall be stored within the territory of the Republic
of Indonesia. No data shall be transferred to servers outside Indonesia
without prior written consent.

4.3 The Provider shall implement industry-standard encryption (AES-256)
for data at rest and TLS 1.3 for data in transit.

4.4 In the event of a data breach, the Provider shall notify the Client
within 24 hours of discovery. The Provider shall bear all costs associated
with breach remediation, customer notification, and regulatory reporting.

4.5 Upon termination, the Provider shall return or destroy all Client data
within 30 days at the Client's discretion.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

5.1 All intellectual property rights in the Platform shall remain with
the Provider. The Client is granted a non-exclusive, non-transferable
license to use the Platform for the duration of this Agreement.

5.2 Any customizations or modifications developed specifically for the
Client shall be jointly owned by both parties.

5.3 The Provider warrants that the Platform does not infringe upon any
third-party intellectual property rights.

6. LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION

6.1 The Provider's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed
the total contract value paid by the Client in the 12 months preceding
the claim.

6.2 Neither party shall be liable for indirect, consequential, or
punitive damages.

6.3 The Provider shall indemnify and hold harmless the Client against
any third-party claims arising from the Provider's breach of this
Agreement, including but not limited to intellectual property infringement,
data breaches, and regulatory penalties.

7. TERMINATION

7.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement with 90 days written notice.

7.2 The Client may terminate immediately if:
(a) The Provider fails to meet SLA requirements for 3 consecutive months
(b) A material data breach occurs due to Provider negligence
(c) The Provider becomes insolvent or enters bankruptcy proceedings

7.3 Upon termination, the Provider shall:
(a) Provide transition assistance for up to 6 months
(b) Return all Client data per Section 4.5
(c) Refund any prepaid amounts for undelivered services

7.4 The following sections survive termination: 4 (Data Protection),
5 (IP), 6 (Liability), and 8 (Confidentiality).

8. CONFIDENTIALITY

8.1 Both parties shall maintain the confidentiality of all proprietary
information exchanged under this Agreement for a period of 5 years
following termination.

8.2 Confidential information may be disclosed if required by law, court
order, or regulatory authority, provided the disclosing party gives
reasonable prior notice.

9. FORCE MAJEURE

9.1 Neither party shall be liable for failure to perform due to events
beyond reasonable control, including natural disasters, war, government
actions, or pandemic-related restrictions.

9.2 The affected party must notify the other within 7 days of the force
majeure event. If the event continues for more than 90 days, either party
may terminate without liability.

10. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

10.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Republic of
Indonesia.

10.2 Any dispute shall first be resolved through mediation. If mediation
fails within 30 days, the dispute shall be referred to arbitration at
the Indonesian National Board of Arbitration (BANI) in Jakarta.

10.3 The arbitration shall be conducted in Bahasa Indonesia and the
arbitral award shall be final and binding.
